# Independent AI Assurance — Product

Independent AI Assurance registers, evaluates, monitors and documents AI systems operated by other organizations. It does not build or optimize the systems it assesses.

## MVP scope

The local MVP provides a multilingual assurance workspace; AI inventory; normalized controls and framework summaries; deterministic demo evaluations; findings and evidence views; monitoring and release gate journeys; compliance readiness, reports, integrations and settings screens.

## Boundary

Assessment results support AI risk, governance and compliance workflows. They do not constitute legal advice, regulatory approval or certification. No real model or customer data is contacted by demo evaluations.

## Users

AI governance, model risk, compliance, security, internal audit, system owners and executive stakeholders. Demo auth currently uses a mock assurance lead session.
